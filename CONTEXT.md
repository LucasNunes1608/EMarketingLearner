# Negócio Digital

A free video course platform teaching Brazilian small entrepreneurs (MEIs, small shop
owners) practical digital skills. It is not monetized and carries no accounts, so most of
its vocabulary describes content and learning rather than customers or subscriptions.

Learner-facing text is Brazilian Portuguese; code, comments and commits are English. Where
a term below has a pt-BR counterpart used in URLs and UI copy, both are given — use the
English term in code and the pt-BR term in anything a learner reads.

## Language

### Cost

**Zero marginal cost**:
The constraint that serving one additional learner costs nothing. The only recurring
expense is the domain, so cost does not rise with audience size.
_Avoid_: cheap, low-cost, budget, affordable

**Fixed cost**:
The annual expense that exists regardless of audience — the domain, and nothing else.
_Avoid_: overhead, running costs

### Learning

**Learner** (`aluno` in copy):
Someone taking a course. No account or identity exists for them; they are anonymous to
the platform.
_Avoid_: user, student, subscriber, customer

**Course** (`curso`):
An ordered set of lessons on a single topic, published as a unit.
_Avoid_: class, training, program, módulo

**Lesson** (`aula`):
One video with written steps, belonging to exactly one course, with at most one worksheet.
_Avoid_: video, episode, module, unit

**Worksheet** (`ficha`):
A printable one-page exercise belonging to one lesson, designed to be filled in by hand.
_Avoid_: handout, exercise, PDF, activity

**Progress**:
The set of lessons a learner has marked complete. It exists on one device only and is
never associated with a person.
_Avoid_: completion state, history, enrollment

**Certificate** (`certificado`):
Printable proof that a learner completed every lesson in a course. It attests to nothing
verifiable and is not issued by any authority.
_Avoid_: diploma, credential, badge
